Dawit Ayanssa

Founder & Lead Photographer

With over a decade of experience behind the lens, I founded Darokel Studio to bridge the gap between fine art and commercial photography. My approach is rooted in minimalism—stripping away visual noise to capture the raw, unadulterated essence of every subject.

We believe that a photograph is more than an image; it is a legacy. Whether we are shooting an editorial campaign, an intimate wedding, or a corporate gala, our philosophy remains the same: elegance through simplicity.
